A virtual portfolio turns investing from an abstract topic into a practical learning exercise.

Instead of simply saying that a company looks interesting, I can write down **why** I think it may perform well, identify the risks, and then track what happens. Over time, the result becomes less important than understanding which parts of the original thesis were right or wrong.

For our Investment Club, this creates a useful framework for discussing diversification, risk, market news, and long-term decision-making.
